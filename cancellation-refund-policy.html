Customers may request cancellation of a subscription or recurring service at any time by contacting us through the contact information provided on TaxPreparerTools.com. Cancellation requests should be submitted before the next billing date to avoid being charged for the following billing period.

Unless otherwise stated at the time of purchase, cancellation will stop future recurring charges but does not automatically provide a refund for amounts already paid.

Refunds

Refund eligibility depends on the specific product or service purchased and the terms presented at the time of purchase.

If a refund is available, customers may request one by contacting us with their name, account information, purchase date, and reason for the request. Approved refunds will generally be issued to the original payment method.

Digital products, subscriptions, or services that have already been accessed or used may not be eligible for a refund, except where required by applicable law or where otherwise stated in the applicable offer.

Billing Errors

If you believe you have been charged incorrectly or have received a duplicate charge, please contact us promptly so we can review the transaction and, where appropriate, issue a correction or refund.

Processing Time

Once a refund is approved, processing times may vary depending on the payment provider and financial institution. Customers should allow several business days for an approved refund to appear on their original payment method.
