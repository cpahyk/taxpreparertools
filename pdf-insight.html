What it checks for

The score isn't a black box. It's built from a short list of concrete checks run against the actual line items on the statement.

RECONCILIATION

Does the math tie out

Each transaction's amount is checked against the change in balance from the line before it. Statements where the numbers don't chain together get flagged rather than trusted.

TRUE REVENUE

Revenue, not just deposits

Transfers between accounts, loan proceeds, and other non-sales deposits are filtered out, so the revenue figure reflects money actually earned by the business.

BALANCE HEALTH

Negative and low-balance days

Every day the account sat below zero, or below a thin cushion, is counted — a better read on cash-flow stress than a single point-in-time balance.

DUPLICATE DETECTION

Repeated line items

The same date, description, and amount showing up more than once is a common sign of an edited or spliced document, and gets called out by line.
