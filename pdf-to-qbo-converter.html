The workflow

Three steps. No re-typing.

01

Open the PDF

Point the app at a bank statement PDF. It auto-detects the bank and reads every transaction line — checks, debits, and credits.

02

Review & reconcile

Every transaction lands in a review table, checked against the statement's own printed totals. Mismatches are flagged before you export, not after.

03

Export the QBO

One click writes a standard QBO file. Import it into QuickBooks Desktop or Online like any bank download.

Questions

Before you subscribe

Is my bank statement data safe?

Yes — the PDF is parsed entirely on your computer. Nothing about its contents is sent anywhere. The only network call the app makes is a license check.

Which banks are supported?

Fifth Third and Climate First have dedicated parsers. Other banks are handled by a universal parser that reads standard statement layouts — review the output before exporting, as with any bank.

Where do I get my license key?

You'll receive it by email after subscribing. Paste it into the app's license screen the first time you run it.

What happens if I cancel?

Your license stays valid through the end of the billing period you already paid for. The app keeps working for parsing and review; exporting requires an active subscription.

Is there a Mac version?

Not yet — reach out and we'll let you know when one's available.
